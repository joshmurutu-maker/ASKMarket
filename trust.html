Pre-launch / prototype disclosures

ASKMarket is being developed as a wholesale and retail marketplace. This page explains what protections are present, what remains incomplete, and what buyers and sellers should not assume.

Important: ASKMarket is not currently represented as a licensed financial institution, regulated investment platform, certified marketplace, or independently audited service. A successful website deployment is not proof of legal approval or provider authorization.

Accounts & identity checks

Account sign-up uses the configured authentication provider when credentials are supplied by the operator. KYC/KYB screens are present, but a verification badge must only be shown after a trusted provider decision is received and checked server-side. A submitted application is not approval.

Verification availability depends on configuration

Payments & wallet

Real deposits, withdrawals, checkout, escrow and payouts are not active. Payment API routes intentionally reject requests until approved provider integrations, ledger controls and verification are complete. Never send money to a person or wallet address claiming to represent ASKMarket.

Real-money transactions disabled

Listings & seller status

Some catalogue entries are demonstration listings. A sample listing or sample seller label is not proof of stock, authenticity, seller identity or delivery capability. Confirm seller details and product terms before making any off-platform arrangement.

Demo content may be displayed

Privacy & data

Account and verification features may process personal data when configured. The privacy notice is still a draft and must be completed with the legal operator identity, provider list, retention schedule, rights contact and cross-border transfer safeguards before public launch.

Privacy notice requires legal review

Orders, shipping & refunds

Do not assume an order has been accepted, paid, shipped or refunded unless the platform and relevant provider confirm it. Delivery estimates, shipping charges and seller-specific return conditions must be disclosed before a live checkout is enabled.

Live fulfilment workflow not confirmed

Investments & earnings

Creator rewards, referrals, advertising income and investor/shareholding features are not guaranteed returns or an offer to invest. No one should transfer funds in expectation of profit or equity through this prototype. Any future securities activity requires separate legal and regulatory review.

No investment offer or guaranteed earnings

How we intend to protect the marketplace

  • Require identity and business checks appropriate to each seller and category, with approval status verified by the server.
  • Keep payment-provider secrets on the server; never ask users for wallet seed phrases or private keys.
  • Use authenticated, signed provider webhooks, idempotency, reconciliation and a properly controlled ledger before enabling payments.
  • Provide clear product descriptions, seller terms, shipping fees, refund rules and dispute handling before checkout.
  • Restrict unlawful, counterfeit, stolen, unsafe and prohibited goods; investigate reports and preserve appropriate audit records.
  • Apply least-privilege access, secure session handling, backups, monitoring, incident response and independent security testing.

Report a concern

For account, listing or transaction concerns, use the support channel displayed inside the live service only after verifying that it belongs to ASKMarket. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, private keys, seed phrases, identity documents or full payment credentials in ordinary messages.

Security disclosure contact: A dedicated verified security-reporting email and escalation contact have not yet been published. This is a launch blocker. Do not post sensitive vulnerability details in public issue trackers.

Legal operator: The registered operating entity, company number, registered address and official complaint contact have not yet been confirmed on this page. This is also a launch blocker.
