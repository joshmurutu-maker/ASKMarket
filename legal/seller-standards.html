Seller Standards

Draft v0.1 · 6 November 2026

1. Verify your business

Provide accurate legal entity, registration, ownership, beneficial-owner, address and authorized-representative details. Approval depends on trusted verification results and may be renewed or suspended.

2. List lawful products

Only list genuine goods you are legally entitled to sell. Provide accurate descriptions, condition, specifications, origin where relevant, safety warnings, price, stock and minimum order quantity.

3. Restricted goods

Do not list unlawful goods, counterfeit or stolen property, weapons, explosives, controlled drugs, hazardous substances, wildlife products, sanctioned goods or items prohibited by law or ASKMarket category rules. Some goods require licences or specialist review.

4. Fulfilment

Maintain accurate stock, meet delivery commitments, package goods safely, provide tracking where available and respond promptly to buyer issues. Do not mark an order delivered without evidence.

5. Honest promotion

Do not fabricate reviews, manipulate rankings or misrepresent sponsored placement. Paid promotion must be clearly labelled.
