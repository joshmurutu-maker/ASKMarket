Trust, safety & legal information

Clear information for buyers and sellers. These draft policies are a starting point—not a claim that the business has completed every legal registration or compliance step.

ACTION REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH

Business identity must be completed

Replace every bracketed field before launch: [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], company registration number, registered address, support email, privacy contact, complaints contact, and any applicable tax or licence details. Do not publish invented company credentials, regulator approvals or certifications. Have Kenyan counsel review these policies and confirm whether the platform operator is a marketplace intermediary, seller, payment service provider, data controller or processor for each activity.

Terms of use

Users must provide accurate account details, keep credentials secure, and comply with applicable law. Sellers are responsible for lawful products, accurate descriptions, stock, pricing, fulfilment, taxes, warranties and customer communications. Prohibited, counterfeit, stolen, unsafe or unlawful goods are not allowed. The platform may suspend listings or accounts for credible safety, fraud or legal concerns, subject to a fair review process.

Unless a separate signed agreement says otherwise, buyers purchase from the named seller—not from the platform operator. The operator's exact role, liability allocation, dispute process and governing-law clauses must be confirmed by counsel before these terms are treated as final.

Prices, delivery fees, taxes and any platform fee must be shown before an order is confirmed. No order, escrow, buyer-protection or payment feature should be represented as active until its backend and operating process are tested and enabled.

Privacy notice

The platform may process account and contact details, business information, listings, order/support records, device and security logs, and verification status. Identity documents or biometric information must be collected only through an approved provider workflow where necessary—not pasted into public listings or ordinary support chat.

Purposes may include account security, marketplace operations, fraud prevention, customer support, legal obligations and optional marketing where a valid basis exists. Service providers may include hosting, database, AI image generation and identity-verification vendors. International transfers, retention periods, lawful bases, controller/processor roles and ODPC registration status must be documented before launch.

Users should be given a clear way to request access, correction, objection or deletion where applicable. Privacy contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL]. Controller: [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME AND ADDRESS].

Returns, refunds & complaints

Product pages must state the seller, item condition, total price, delivery terms, applicable warranty and return/refund conditions before purchase. Nothing in a seller policy removes mandatory rights under applicable consumer law. Sellers must respond to genuine complaints and cooperate with lawful remedies, including repair, replacement or refund where required.

Complaint contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Include order reference, seller name, product, issue and requested remedy. Publish response times and escalation steps only after the team can consistently meet them. No claim of buyer protection or guaranteed refunds should be made until a funded and operational process exists.

Seller standards

  • List only goods the seller is legally entitled to sell.
  • Use accurate pricing, quantity, condition, origin, specifications and delivery estimates.
  • Do not manipulate reviews, rankings, stock or competitor prices.
  • Disclose sponsored placements and commercial relationships.
  • Keep proof of sourcing and fulfilment where appropriate.
  • Respond promptly to safety, authenticity and customer complaints.

AI-generated product images

Marketplace product artwork may be generated by AI and is clearly labelled. It is illustrative and is not independent evidence of the actual item's condition, authenticity, size, colour, contents or availability. Sellers remain responsible for ensuring every image and description is accurate and must not use AI artwork to conceal defects, imply an unearned certification or depict a brand they are not authorised to use. A real-item photograph should be added where needed to resolve material uncertainty.

Payments & verification status

Do not send money to personal numbers or wallet addresses based on a chat message. Only use payment methods displayed in an enabled official checkout. As of this draft, payment, payout and crypto-custody functions are not represented as live. A KYC/KYB screen being opened or completed does not mean an account is approved; only a verified provider result recorded by the platform may support that status.
