Returns and Refunds

Draft v0.1 · 6 November 2026

1. Before ordering

Review the product, seller, quantity, minimum order, price, delivery estimate, shipping, taxes and seller-specific conditions before checkout.

2. Report an issue

Report non-delivery, damage, wrong items, missing quantities or material mismatch using the order support channel. Include the order reference and relevant evidence; do not send identity documents in ordinary support messages.

3. Resolution

Depending on the issue and applicable law, resolution may include replacement, repair, partial refund, full refund or cancellation. Record the decision and reason.

4. Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent through the original payment method or another lawful provider-supported method. Posting times depend on the payment provider and financial institution; no refund is complete until provider confirmation.

5. Seller responsibilities

Sellers must comply with consumer protection, product safety, warranty, tax and return obligations. Seller terms cannot remove mandatory statutory rights.
