Privacy Notice

Draft v0.1 · 6 November 2026 · Proposed initial market: Kenya

1. Controller identity

The ASKMarket operating company will be the data controller. Its registered name, address, privacy contact and ODPC registration applicability must be confirmed before launch.

2. Information processed

  • Account name, email, phone, role and business details.
  • Business registration, ownership and verification status.
  • Product, order, delivery, support and communication records.
  • Payment references, amounts, refunds and ledger entries; full card/bank credentials remain with the payment provider.
  • Security, device, diagnostic and consent records.

3. Purposes

Account management, KYC/KYB, marketplace operations, order fulfilment, payment reconciliation, fraud prevention, customer support, legal compliance and consent-based marketing.

4. Providers and transfers

Hosting, authentication, verification, payment, delivery, analytics and advertising providers may process data. Their names, processing locations, contractual roles and cross-border safeguards must be listed before launch.

5. Retention and security

Keep data only as long as needed for documented purposes or legal requirements. Use least-privilege access, encryption, audit logging, backups and incident response. Do not store identity documents or biometrics in the public frontend.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or portability and withdraw consent where applicable. Insert the official privacy contact and complaint process, including ODPC information where appropriate.

7. Cookies and advertising

Non-essential analytics and advertising must not activate until appropriate consent and preference controls are implemented. Paid placements must be labelled.
