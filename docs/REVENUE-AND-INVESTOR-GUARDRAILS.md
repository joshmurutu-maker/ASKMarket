# Revenue, creator earnings and investor guardrails

## Platform revenue
ASKMarket can earn from a combination of disclosed completed-order service fees, seller subscriptions, optional promoted listings, direct sponsorships and eligible advertising inventory. Each charge must be visible before the seller or buyer commits.

## Creator earnings
A creator, referrer or marketplace partner may receive a contractual share of eligible net platform fees. The formula must be documented, visible in a statement and calculated only after the underlying order settles. Refunds, chargebacks, taxes, provider fees and fraud reversals must be accounted for. A referral bonus is a marketing expense, not an investment return.

## Funds cycle
Customer money is not ASKMarket revenue merely because it passes through a payment provider. Customer funds, seller proceeds, refunds and platform fees must remain separately identified in provider settlement reporting and a balanced double-entry ledger. Never pay an earlier participant using deposits from later participants. Never promise fixed or guaranteed returns.

## Advertising
All paid placements must be labelled Sponsored/Advertisement. Ad network approval and policy compliance are required. No incentivized clicks, click exchanges, fabricated traffic or misleading ad placement.

## Shareholding
Shareholders own shares in the legal company, not balances in customer wallets. Investor invitations must be non-binding until a registered company, approved materials, legal review and any required Kenyan securities approvals are in place. No public share sale, tokenized equity, dividend promise or investment collection should be enabled from the prototype.

## Decisions required before production
- Legal entity and registered operating name.
- Payment provider contract supporting Kenya marketplace split settlement and seller payouts.
- Commission schedule, seller agreement, refund and dispute policies.
- Approved investor-relations process and counsel-reviewed share documentation.
